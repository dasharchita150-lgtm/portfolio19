# Portfolio website PRD

## Theme Header
### Modern professional portfolio

## Purpose
Create a portfolio website that presents the developer’s projects, background, skills, and education clearly and confidently for recruiters, visitors, and interviewers.

## Audience
- Recruiters evaluating technical and product fit.
- Visitors who want to understand the developer quickly.
- Interviewers who need a polished overview of skills and experience.

## Main Goals
- Explain the projects the developer has worked on.
- Introduce who the developer is in a clear, memorable way.
- Highlight the strongest project prominently.
- Make skills and strengths easy to scan.
- Provide a clear contact action for follow-up.

## Required sections
- Hero / introduction
- About section
- Projects section
- Skills section
- Education section
- Contact section

## Required behaviour
- Must work well on mobile and desktop.
- Must be easy to navigate.
- Must load quickly and feel lightweight.
- Project and contact links should work correctly.
- Content should be easy to understand at a glance.
- Layout should adapt cleanly to smaller screens.

## Visual theme
- Deep navy background with subtle blue gradients.
- Professional blue accent color for emphasis.
- Clean sans-serif typography with strong hierarchy.
- Card-based layout with soft borders, balanced spacing, and minimal decoration.

## Content priorities
1. Introduce the person quickly and confidently.
2. Show the strongest project early.
3. Make skills and education simple to scan.
4. Include a visible contact prompt.
