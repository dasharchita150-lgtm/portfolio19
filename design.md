# Design Document

## Theme Header
### Modern professional portfolio

## Design goals
- Present the developer clearly and confidently.
- Keep the layout readable on mobile and desktop.
- Use a polished visual system that supports recruiters and interviewers.

## Visual direction
- Background: deep navy with subtle blue gradients.
- Accent: calm electric blue for clarity and trust.
- Typography: clean sans-serif with strong hierarchy.
- Cards: soft borders, layered surfaces, and light shadows.

## Theme headers
### Hero section
A focused intro that explains who the person is, what they build, and what action the visitor should take next.

### About section
A concise summary that emphasizes product thinking, communication, and practical execution.

### Project section
A card-based layout that highlights featured work and makes details easy to scan.

### Skills and education sections
Simple grouped blocks and a timeline for quick scanning without visual clutter.

### Contact section
A high-visibility CTA that invites recruiters, clients, or collaborators to reach out.
